How do I book a VoltRide? +

Choose your ride type, add your pickup and destination, then select the vehicle that fits your journey. You’ll see the exact estimate before you confirm.

Yes. Our growing fleet is electric-first, with every vehicle selected for comfort, range, and a low-noise city experience.

Can I schedule a ride for later? +

Absolutely. Choose your date and time in the booking panel and we’ll reserve the best available vehicle for your window.
